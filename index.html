How it works

Place a bid: Add a bid to “mine” an item. If no other user locks the item, we’ll reach out via TikTok chat to get more information.

Lock an item: Lock an item to make it yours. We’ll reach out via TikTok chat—please look out for our direct message so we can get your information.

Payment & shipping: Payment must be completed first. Shipping fees depend on your location. Once payment is confirmed, we’ll ship the item to you.

We’ll contact you directly via TikTok chat after a bid or lock is placed. Please make sure your TikTok messages are accessible.
